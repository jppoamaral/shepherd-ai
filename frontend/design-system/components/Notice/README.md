A banner that says what happened and what to do next, in two short lines: a bold lead ("1 reminder didn't send"), then the next step ("Try again or copy it."). **Info** (sage) is for facts the leader can act on; **error** (`status-alert-*`) is only for something that failed and carries `role="alert"`.

Always pair the icon with the words, and always offer a way forward (Try again, Copy). Never blame the leader and never use more than one error banner per screen.
